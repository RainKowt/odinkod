# OneCode real-footage short format

The two supplied videos are style references only. Do not republish their footage, audio, captions, or watermark.

## Primary format: fast commentary

- 9:16, 1080x1920, 25-45 seconds.
- Show the claim or surprise in the first 1.5 seconds.
- Cut on every new idea, normally every 1.5-3 seconds.
- Use genuine footage, screen recordings, product close-ups, maps, posts, and receipts.
- Keep captions to 2-6 words, white bold type with a heavy black outline.
- Use a blurred duplicate behind landscape clips; never stretch them.
- End with a useful payoff, then a short OneCode CTA. Do not interrupt the story with an ad.

## Secondary format: emotional UGC

- Use 4-8 second shots and let the reaction play out.
- Keep overlays minimal and preserve natural sound where it carries the moment.
- Use for gifts, price wins, before/after savings, and customer stories.

## Source rules

Every scene must have a source URL and a recorded permission or license. Approved inputs are footage made by OneCode, footage supplied with permission, licensed stock, public-domain material, and official assets whose terms allow the intended use. A public post is not automatically reusable. Do not remove watermarks or present another creator's work as OneCode's.

`render-source-short.mjs` refuses to render a scene if either `source` or `rights` is absent.
