Privacy, terms & refunds

Effective September 5, 2026. This page describes the OneCode digital membership offered through this website.

Membership and billing

OneCode provides access to a catalog of third-party promotional codes and product links. One promotional code may be revealed without charge. The paid membership is $5 USD per month and renews automatically until canceled. The price and renewal terms are shown again before checkout.

Payments and recurring billing are processed by lava.top. OneCode does not receive or store full payment-card details. You can manage or cancel renewal through your lava.top purchases account. Cancellation stops future renewals and does not remove access already paid for.

Offer terms

Promotional codes, prices, availability, shipping, eligibility and expiration dates are controlled by third-party merchants. They may change without notice. Always confirm the final price and conditions on the merchant website before purchasing. OneCode does not guarantee savings, inventory or acceptance of a code.

Some outbound links are affiliate links. OneCode may receive a commission from qualifying purchases without increasing the price charged by the merchant.

Refunds

Because membership provides immediate access to digital content, completed membership charges are generally non-refundable except where required by applicable law or when the service was not supplied. Billing and refund requests should be submitted from the purchase in your lava.top account so the payment can be identified securely.

Privacy

OneCode uses a random browser session identifier to enforce the free selection and membership access. If you purchase membership, your email address and payment status are processed to provide access and receipts. Payment details are processed by lava.top. Technical logs may be processed by the hosting provider to operate and secure the service.

We do not sell personal information. Data is retained only as needed to provide access, maintain transaction records, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. You can request billing-related support or exercise applicable data rights through the support tools attached to your lava.top purchase.

Acceptable use and changes

Do not attempt to bypass access controls, abuse merchant offers or use the service unlawfully. We may remove unavailable offers and update these terms as the service changes. Material changes apply prospectively and will be reflected by a new effective date.
